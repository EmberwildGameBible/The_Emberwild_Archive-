# Parasilmolous

**Parasilmolous** *(adjective)*  
**Pronunciation:** para-sil-MOL-us

**Definition:** exhibiting an abnormal, parallel growth or material state that develops alongside, infiltrates, and progressively alters ordinary matter, particularly where the affected material acquires crystalline, fibrous, mineral-like, or mold-like structural characteristics.

A **parasilmolous** transformation differs from an ordinary surface deposit in that the secondary state becomes structurally continuous with, or progressively replaces, portions of the original substrate.

## Derived forms

**Parasilmolosis** *(noun)* — a process or condition involving parasilmolous transformation.

**Parasilmolic** *(adjective)* — relating to, resulting from, or exhibiting parasilmolosis.

**Parasilmorph** *(noun)* — a structure exhibiting parasilmolous characteristics.

## Etymology

Formed from **para-**, the Greek-derived combining form associated with *beside*, *alongside*, *beyond*, and *abnormal*; the medial construction **silmol-**, suggestive of siliceous/mineral and molecular structure; and **-ous**, meaning *characterized by* or *possessing*.
